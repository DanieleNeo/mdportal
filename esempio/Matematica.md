# Formule matematiche

Una formula inline: $\Delta\theta = \theta_{ref} - \theta_{native}$.

$$
E' = E\cos\Delta\theta + N\sin\Delta\theta
$$

$$
\begin{bmatrix}E_{ref}\\N_{ref}\end{bmatrix}
\approx s R(\phi)\begin{bmatrix}E_{native}\\N_{native}\end{bmatrix}
$$

Una frazione: \(\frac{a}{b}\). Codice letterale: `$x$`.
